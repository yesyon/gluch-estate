About Us

Coming home, one property at a time

Gluch Real Estate Ltd. is a family-run, licensed brokerage (No. 312103135) based in Ra'anana. We handle sales, lettings and property management across Ra'anana, Jerusalem, Tel Aviv and coastal Israel.

Many of our clients are buying in Israel from abroad. Others have just arrived, and some have lived here for decades. We look after each of them as if the property were our own.

Our story

For the past 18 years, Gluch Real Estate has built a boutique agency around a simple idea: focus on Israel's key locations for the foreign market, know them better than anyone, and get in ahead of the crowd. We've stayed deliberately focused on the top-performing locations across the country, while working to identify strong but underappreciated areas before the wider market catches on.

Our mission

Connecting local and diaspora buyers — especially from Canada and North America — with new-build opportunities across Israel, backed by financing access and management support that make ownership simple from a distance.

Our reputation is our clients' to tell

We'd rather not describe ourselves with words every agency uses. Most of our business comes from people who have worked with us before and the people they send our way. We think that says more than a mission statement, so we let our clients speak for us.

Why we do this

More than a transaction

Helping people buy a home is rewarding anywhere. Here it means something more. Many of the families we work with are part of the return to our ancient homeland. Some are planting roots for children and grandchildren, and some are finally making a lifelong hope real. Being part of that, deal by deal and family by family, is the reason we come to work.
