About Us

A licensed brokerage built around new developments

Gluch Real Estate Ltd. is a licensed brokerage (No. 312103135) based in Ra'anana, handling sales, lettings and property management across Ra'anana, Tel Aviv, Jerusalem and coastal Israel.

[Photo: team / office]

Our story

For the past 17 years, Gluch Real Estate has built a boutique agency around a simple idea: focus on Israel's key locations for the foreign market, know them better than anyone, and get in ahead of the crowd. We've stayed deliberately focused on the top-performing locations across the country, while working to identify strong but underappreciated areas before the wider market catches on.

Our mission

Connecting local and diaspora buyers — especially from Canada and North America — with new-build opportunities across Israel, backed by financing access and management support that make ownership simple from a distance.

Our values

Transparency and hands-on local expertise, in every transaction — from first inquiry through closing and ongoing management — and long-term relationships with the owners and tenants we work with.
