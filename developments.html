Dolev — HaGalil 27, Ra'anana

Prices starting from NIS 2,990,000

A boutique luxury development on HaGalil and Maccabi streets in the heart of Ra'anana, designed by architects Gal Tevet and Rod Mark. The project offers 93 four- and five-room apartments, garden apartments and penthouses, with two entrances per building and two apartments per floor.

  • Designer lobby, quiet MRL elevators with Shabbat mode
  • Underground parking prepared for EV charging, private storage units
  • VRF air conditioning with room-by-room control
  • Porcelain flooring, double-glazed aluminium windows, electric shutters
  • Thermal and acoustic insulation, colour video intercom
  • Walking distance to parks, top-rated schools, cafés and the Rananim mall; close to Ra'anana Country Club and Meir Hospital
