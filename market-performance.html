Drivers of continued growth

Limited land supply

Israel is a small, densely populated country with tight zoning and planning constraints, which has historically supported long-term price appreciation in high-demand areas.

Population growth

Steady natural population growth, together with ongoing aliyah from North America and elsewhere, continues to drive housing demand in the locations we focus on.

Economic resilience

A diversified, tech-driven economy has helped Israeli real estate hold value through economic cycles that have hit other markets harder.
